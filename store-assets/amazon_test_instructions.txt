FIRST LAUNCH
------------

2. Onboarding (first run only):

   - On Terms & Conditions: accept the checkbox, then proceed to Login.
3. Login:
   - Enter country code: 1
   - Enter phone number: 00
   - Tap Submit.
   - Enter auth code: 12345
   - Tap Submit.
4. You will land on the Home dashboard.


RECOMMENDED TEST FLOW (5–10 minutes)
------------------------------------
Step 1 — Home dashboard
  • Confirm two main sections are visible: Chats and History.

Step 2 — Browse channels
  • Select "Open Chats" (or focus the Chats card and press OK/Enter).
  • A grid of sample channels appears (e.g. Movies Channel, Series Hub).
  • Open "Movies Channel" (first row, first item).

Step 3 — Browse media library
  • A grid of sample video titles is shown.
  • Select any title (e.g. "Unrated 1000 - Chat 1") to open Details.

Step 4 — Title details
  • Verify poster image, synopsis, and file metadata load.
  • Movie info may load from TMDB (requires network).

Step 5 — Download and play
  • Select Download on the bottom action bar.
  • Wait for download to complete (test video is fetched from a public
    sample URL; progress bar reaches 100%).
  • When complete, Play and Full Screen buttons appear.
  • Select Play or Full Screen — playback opens in the built-in player.
  • No separate VLC install is required for the test account.

Step 6 — History (optional)
  • Press Back to return to previous screens.
  • From Home, open History.
  • Confirm viewed/downloaded titles appear in the list.

TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
• Blank channel/message grids after fresh install:
  Wait 30–60 seconds on first launch for local data to finish seeding,
  then restart the app and log in again with the test account.

• Download fails:
  Confirm the device has internet access and retry Download.

• Play button does not appear:
  Wait until the download progress bar shows 100% and the file status
  shows "Downloaded".

• Login rejected:
  Use exactly country code 1, phone 00, code 12345 (not a real number).
